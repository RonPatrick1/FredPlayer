## Full description

FredPlayer is a private music player for MP3 and FLAC libraries you choose.
Enjoy synchronized waveform and spectrum visuals, consistent playback levels,
background playback, playlists, and optional Bluetooth visual-delay
calibration.

Connect FredPlayer to your own private Fred Server to browse your library,
stream progressively, reuse server-computed visualization and leveling data,
and share playlists between your devices. Deleting a shared playlist from a
device does not remove its independent server copy.

FredPlayer also supports Android Auto media browsing and voice search. The app
contains no ads, analytics, or tracking and does not require an account.

Users must supply or connect audio they are authorized to use. A Fred Server is
optional and is not operated as a public hosted service by Silveron Studios.
